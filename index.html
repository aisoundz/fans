How it works

We only play the gaps

01
The set is sacred
No question ever fires while a song is playing. The game lives in the five dead spots a show already has — doors, the changeover, the costume break, the encore wait, and the walk-out.
02
You answer about the show you are standing in
What opened it. How many dancers. What he wore. Nothing you can look up — only things you can see from where you are.
03
The artist pays out, not a bookmaker
The setlist off the stage. Soundcheck in the next city. Your name on the wall between sets. Entry is free and always will be.
04
Then it follows you home
The songs you answered about come back on Spotify next week and ask you again. That is not a gimmick — it is the part that makes the night stick.
